# Quick Start Guide

## Get Started in 3 Minutes

### 1. Create Your Account (30 seconds)
1. Open the application
2. Click **"Don't have an account? Sign up"**
3. Fill in:
   - Your email
   - A password (min 6 characters)
   - Business name (optional)
4. Click **"Create Account"**

✅ You're now logged in!

### 2. Add Your First Customer (1 minute)
1. Go to **"Customers"** page
2. Click **"Add Customer"**
3. Enter customer details:
   - Name
   - Email (use your own email for testing)
   - Phone (optional)
4. Click **"Save"**

✅ Customer added!

### 3. Send Your First Email (1.5 minutes)
1. Go to **"Email Campaigns"**
2. Click **"New Campaign"**
3. Fill in:
   - Campaign Name: "Test Email"
   - Subject: "Hello from QuantiEdge"
   - Content: "Hi {{firstName}}, this is a test email!"
4. Select your customer as recipient
5. Click **"Send Now"**

✅ Email sent! Check your inbox (might be in spam folder)
